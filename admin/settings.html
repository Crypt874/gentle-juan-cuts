    Confirmation emails

    When staff confirm an appointment and the customer left an email address, a confirmation email goes to them.

    • Automatic emails are off (default): your email app opens with the message already written. Staff just press Send.
    • Automatic emails are on: the email is sent for you, with no extra step. This needs a free EmailJS account.

    In EmailJS, create a template with To email set to {{to_email}}, subject {{subject}} and body {{message}}.

    Back up my data

    Saves a copy of everything in the shop system to your computer.

    1. Click Download backup.
    2. A file is saved in your Downloads folder.
    3. Keep that file safe, for example on a flash drive or in Google Drive.

    Restore from a backup

    Use this only if data was lost or changed by mistake. It puts back everything saved in your backup file.

    Nothing is deleted. We also save a copy of your current data first, just in case.
